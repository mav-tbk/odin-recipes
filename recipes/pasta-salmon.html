Pasta with Salmon

Home

Description

This recipe mixes two things that always taste good. This combination might still be overlooked a little. Everybody can make it but my Gran does it the best.

Ingredients

  • pasta
  • salmon
  • dill sauce

Steps

  1. Cook the pasta.
  2. Fry the salmon.
  3. Finally, the ready-made sauce. Follow instructions on packaging.
  4. Mix everything together and add some green stuff if you like.
