Döner

Home

Description

This food is rather Turkish but it is highly popular in Germany. You should try it.

Ingredients

  • flatbread
  • meat
  • sauce of your choice: garlic, herbs, spicy
  • salad
  • onions
  • more vegetabels

Steps

  1. Fry the meat.
  2. Heat up the bread.
  3. First, spread sauce on bread.
  4. Add salad and onions and vegetables.
  5. Add meat.
